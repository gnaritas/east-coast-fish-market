Before you drive over

When do you open?

Grand opening weekend runs Friday through Sunday, November 27–29, from 10:30am to 6:00pm each day.

Where exactly are you?

248 Oxford Road, Units I & J, Oxford, CT 06478. Tap “Get directions” above and your car will know.

What seafood will you have fresh?

Lobster, salmon, mussels, oysters, scallops, and cod on the counter — the board follows the market, so call if you’re after one thing in particular.

Is the fried stuff really made to order?

It is. The shrimp, clams, and whole-bellies hit the fryer when you order them. Lobster rolls, grilled salmon, and fish & chips come off the same hot line.

Who’s behind the counter?

The crew from East Coast Clambakes, who have run clambake and barbecue catering across four states for more than twenty years. Read the story →

What if I can’t make the opening weekend?

The market keeps going after the grand opening — call (203) 304-4944 or use the contact page and we’ll steer you right.

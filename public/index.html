The team behind the market

Twenty years of clambakes, now on Oxford Road

East Coast Fish Market comes from East Coast Clambakes — a Connecticut catering crew that has run clambakes and barbecues across Connecticut, Massachusetts, Rhode Island, and New York for more than twenty years. Now the same kitchen sets up its own seafood counter: the fresh case, the fryer, and the recipes that fed those parties, ready to feed your week.

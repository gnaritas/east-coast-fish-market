About the market

Twenty Years of Clambakes.
A Brand-New Counter.

East Coast Fish Market is the fresh Seafood storefront from a catering crew that has cooked the coast for two decades.

Opening hours & directions Call (203) 304-4944

East Coast Fish Market comes from East Coast Clambakes — a Connecticut catering company that has spent more than twenty years running clambakes and barbecues across Connecticut, Massachusetts, Rhode Island, and New York. Parties, backyards, big events: the seafood, the fire, the timing — that world is what the crew knows best.

Now the crew is putting that experience behind a counter of its own on Oxford Road. The sourcing is the sourcing. The cooking is the cooking. The difference is that you don’t need an invitation or an event date to get any of it — walk in, order from the case or the board, and dinner’s done.

Twenty-plus years, four states

Clambake and barbecue catering from Connecticut to New York, since long before this storefront existed. That catering side keeps running, by the way — planning an event? Reach the crew at eastcoastclambakes.com.

What travels into the market

Two things, mostly: a habit of picking shellfish and fish well, and a kitchen that cooks them hot instead of holding them warm. That’s the whole pitch, and it’s why the board reads like a clambake that never has to end.
